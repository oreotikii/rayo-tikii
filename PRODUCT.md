# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and decision-makers at startups and growth-stage businesses evaluating whether to hire Tikii as a digital product partner. They arrive with a concrete project in mind — a new product, a rebrand, a platform rebuild — and need to quickly answer three questions: *Can Tikii do what I need? Are they the right creative fit? Can I trust them?*

They're pressed for time, often skeptical of agency-speak, and make judgements in seconds. The site itself is audition footage.

## Product Purpose

Tikii is a full-stack digital product studio: strategy, brand, design, and engineering under one roof. The website exists to win clients — not by describing what Tikii does, but by demonstrating it. Every animation, every layout decision, every interaction is proof of capability. The ideal outcome is a founder finishing the scroll and thinking "I want *this* quality on my product."

Real client work (Chandrani Pearls, Premiere Realty, NMC Tools, Fanatic, Anuschka, Absorbia, etc.) grounds the creative ambition in commercial credibility.

## Brand Personality

Bold · Playful · Inventive

Tikii leads with imagination. The brand has energy — it surprises without being weird, it impresses without being cold. There's warmth under the confidence: a studio that's genuinely excited about the work, not just professionally capable of it. The voice is self-assured but human; never corporate, never try-hard.

## Anti-references

- **Brutalist / raw-type aesthetics**: Heavy Helvetica, intentional misalignment, deliberate ugliness as a style. Tikii is inventive, not abrasive.
- **Generic SaaS startup templates**: Hero metrics, gradient text, identical card grids, eyebrows on every section. The template-industry reflex.
- **Dark neon-glow agency excess**: All effects and no substance. Purple/lime exist in the system but as purposeful choices, not decoration for decoration's sake.
- **Conservative corporate agency**: Navy blue, stock photography, safe layouts. No creative risk, no reason to remember them.

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the proof.** Don't describe creativity — demonstrate it. The site's own craft is the portfolio piece. Every section should make a decision that a lesser agency wouldn't.
2. **Bold without brutalism.** Strong color, expressive type, committed composition — purposeful, not raw for raw's sake. Wit over aggression.
3. **Playfulness with precision.** Energy and surprise in motion and layout, executed with technical rigor. Delight is not an accident.
4. **Warmth under the confidence.** Founders hiring a studio need to feel partnership, not just performance. The brand has charm and personality alongside its capability.
5. **Invention over template.** Every section earns its structure. No scaffolding by reflex, no patterns because "agency sites do this."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal compliance standard required, but the site must be genuinely usable: readable text contrast (minimum 4.5:1 for body), visible focus states for keyboard navigation, no motion that can't be suppressed with `prefers-reduced-motion`. Avoid obvious barriers; the audience is broad enough to include users with visual or motor differences.
